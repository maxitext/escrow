FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Bond for Deed?

A Bond for Deed (also called a Contract for Deed or Land Contract) is a type of seller financing where the buyer makes payments directly to the seller over time. The seller retains legal title to the property until the buyer completes all payments, at which point the deed is transferred to the buyer.

How does your mortgage servicing work?

We collect monthly payments from borrowers, allocate funds between principal and interest, manage escrow accounts for taxes and insurance, provide monthly statements, handle annual tax reporting, and coordinate payoff requests. We serve as a neutral third party to ensure all parties are protected.

What are your fees for escrow services?

Our fees vary depending on the type of service and complexity of the transaction. We offer competitive, transparent pricing with no hidden costs. Contact us for a detailed quote based on your specific needs.

How long does the escrow process take?

The timeline varies depending on the type of transaction and how quickly we receive all necessary documentation. Typically, we can complete setup within 5-10 business days once we have all required information and documents.

Can I make payments online?

Yes! We offer multiple payment options including online payments, automatic ACH withdrawals, check payments, and wire transfers. You can set up automatic payments for convenience and peace of mind.

What happens if a payment is late?

We follow the terms outlined in your contract. Typically, there is a grace period, after which late fees may apply. We'll communicate with both parties and work to resolve any payment issues promptly and professionally.

Do you handle tax and insurance escrow?

Yes, we can manage escrow accounts for property taxes and insurance. We collect these funds as part of your monthly payment, monitor due dates, and ensure timely payment to the appropriate entities.

How do I request a payoff quote?

You can request a payoff quote by contacting us via phone, email, or through your online account portal. We'll provide a detailed payoff statement showing the exact amount needed to satisfy the obligation, including the expiration date of the quote.

What states do you service?

We primarily serve clients nationwide for most of our escrow services. However, some services may be limited based on state-specific regulations. Contact us to discuss your specific location and needs.
