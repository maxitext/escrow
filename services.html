Bond for Deed Contracts

Expert handling of Bond for Deed transactions from contract creation to final closing.

A Bond for Deed (also known as a Contract for Deed or Land Contract) is a seller-financed agreement where the buyer makes payments directly to the seller over time. We specialize in managing these transactions with complete professionalism.

What We Handle:

  • Contract review and preparation
  • Title search and insurance coordination
  • Payment collection and disbursement
  • Tax and insurance escrow management
  • Monthly account statements
  • Deed recording upon contract completion
  • Default management and legal compliance

Private Mortgage Servicing

Complete management of privately held mortgages with professional accuracy and transparency.

When you hold a private mortgage note, proper servicing is essential. We provide comprehensive mortgage servicing that protects both lenders and borrowers while ensuring full compliance with regulations.

Our Services Include:

  • Monthly payment collection and processing
  • Principal and interest allocation
  • Escrow account management (taxes & insurance)
  • Detailed monthly statements
  • Annual tax reporting (1098 forms)
  • Payoff calculations and processing
  • Late payment tracking and communication
  • Lien release coordination

Lease Purchase Agreements

Secure management of lease-to-own transactions with proper documentation and oversight.

Lease purchase agreements allow tenants to rent a property with the option to purchase it at a later date. These arrangements require careful management to protect both parties' interests.

How We Help:

  • Agreement review and verification
  • Rent and option payment collection
  • Rent credit tracking and accounting
  • Option period monitoring
  • Purchase closing coordination
  • Documentation and recordkeeping
  • Buyer and seller communication
Discuss Your Lease Purchase

Wrap-Around Mortgages

Expert management of complex wrap-around mortgage structures.

A wrap-around mortgage is a form of seller financing where the seller maintains their existing mortgage while providing financing to the buyer. This requires specialized servicing to ensure all parties are protected.

Our Expertise:

  • Dual payment management
  • Underlying loan monitoring and payment
  • Spread calculation and accounting
  • Default prevention and notification
  • Comprehensive financial reporting
  • Due-on-sale clause management
  • Payoff coordination for both loans
Contact Us About Wrap Mortgages

Installment Option Contracts

Professional servicing of installment sale agreements with proper financial oversight.

Installment option contracts allow buyers to purchase property through a series of payments with an option to complete the purchase. We ensure these agreements are financially sound and properly enforced.

Services Provided:

  • Contract documentation and setup
  • Installment payment collection
  • Payment allocation and tracking
  • Option period management
  • Financial statement generation
  • Exercise of option processing
  • Default procedures and remedies
  • Final closing coordination
